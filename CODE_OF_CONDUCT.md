# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Trellara to be a focused, welcoming place where people can discuss difficult engineering
problems without making participation difficult for one another. We commit to providing a
harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than people.
- Assume good intent while still naming concrete risks and failures.
- Give credit, accept correction, and make room for people with less context.
- Protect private information and redact credentials, customer data, and sensitive operational
  details from public discussions.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, release claims, and community boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring someone to disclose credentials, customer data, employer information, or security
  details in public.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project spaces,
community events, and other spaces where someone represents Trellara or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct tied to GitHub content, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant item so a
private record reaches GitHub's moderation team. When it is safe to discuss the concern publicly,
you may also notify `@pulkitbhanot` with a minimal issue that does not disclose private information.
Do not publish sensitive evidence or personal details merely to make a report.

Project maintainers will review reports fairly and in context. They may edit or remove content,
close or lock conversations, reject contributions, issue a private or public warning, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of
interest when handling a report and will protect reporter privacy as far as the available channel
allows.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately. Retaliation against a
reporter or participant in an investigation is itself a violation of this policy.

## Attribution

2.1, and adapted for the current Trellara community and its available reporting channels.
